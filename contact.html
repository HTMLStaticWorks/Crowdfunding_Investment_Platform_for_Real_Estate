Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common queries from our investors.

What is the minimum investment amount?
The minimum investment amount varies by property and offering, but typically starts at $10,000 for standard commercial properties and $25,000 for premium institutional-grade assets. Check individual property listings for specific details.
Do I need to be an accredited investor?
Yes, currently most of our high-yield commercial real estate offerings are open only to accredited investors as per SEC Regulation D guidelines. We are actively working on expanding to non-accredited investors in the future.
How and when are distributions paid?
Distributions are typically paid out on a quarterly basis, derived from the rental income of the properties in your portfolio. Proceeds from property sales or refinances are distributed upon the completion of those events.
When will I receive my tax documents?
Schedule K-1 tax forms for your investments are generally finalized and uploaded to your secure investor dashboard by March 15th of the following tax year.
